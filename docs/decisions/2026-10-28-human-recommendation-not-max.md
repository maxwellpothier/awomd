# Human recommendation, not Max

AWOMD's foundation is human music recommendation in place of the algorithm, not
Max's own persona: the lead record is whatever was actually on the desk that week,
from any decade, and other people can take over the desk. Growth to
Fantano/AOTY/RYM scale comes from outside the inbox: Instagram Reels pulled from
each issue, fan communities for each issue's lead record, and guest takeovers whose
artists bring their own audiences, reached through publicists because label-backed
artists don't read DMs. Readers' picks stay out of the letter; replies feed Max's
listening. Rejected: a personality brand like The Needle Drop, because it caps the
letter at one voice, and new-release verdicts, which Fantano already owns.
