*[I Love My Computer](https://open.spotify.com/album/77CZUF57sYqgtznUe3OikQ)* came out last year, and I can't stop going back to it.

It's technically the debut album from
[Ninajirachi](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3MekbRujJg5VZThubOlrkR) (she's
released music for a long time, just not an "album"). After release, and I
guess the singles as part of the rollout too, she had a bit of a breakout year
last year, and hopefully she continues to break out.

Nina is one of my favorite musicians at the moment. She really embodies the
idea of "follow what you're passionate about and practice, practice,
practice". This album is all about that.

Although it's very different than the rest of the album (basically a ballad),
I think **[Sing Good](https://open.spotify.com/track/3n2t35gDOHQS0NMtxKKkZq)** is the best one to start with, because the lyrics set
the tone. Nina is a role model to me because of the sentiment in this song,
which spreads through the rest of the album.

I read it as "I'm not good at this now, but I'm really passionate about it.
Even though I'm going to struggle, I'm going to keep pushing.", said in a much
more poetic way.

**[iPod Touch](https://open.spotify.com/track/1xqT27jSG1Y15vOXfsV0gv)** is probably the song I *should* recommend to start with, but I
think **[London Song](https://open.spotify.com/track/42a9PusvgFnyH0WgdCn6PV)** might be my favorite if I had to choose. Choosing a
second favorite would be impossible.
</Album>

<Section title="Give It a Day">

New one to me from earlier this year, that I remembered earlier this week and
have also kept in heavy rotation:

<Album
  artist="Saint Etienne"
  title="Foxbase Alpha"
  year={1991}
  cover="/covers/saint-etienne-foxbase-alpha.jpg"
  href="https://open.spotify.com/album/3NWRCkCxGD8lf6sBqyChLz"
  pills={["indie dance", "downtempo", "indie pop"]}
>
It's basically really chill european club music, and sounds way ahead of its
time. Very cool blend of indie dance with 1960's girl pop.

Standout is the [Neil Young](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6v8FB84lnmJs434UJf2Mrm) cover
**[Only Love Can Break Your Heart](https://open.spotify.com/track/2YawqESvdpHBZoafDP6i6U)**.

Hearing that, if all these words are familiar to you, you're likely hesitant.
Potentially thinking I have no credibility for recommending a dance cover of
one of Neil's more intimate songs. But turn it on. You'll listen once through
and likely not like it.

Give it a day and it'll click. At least that's what happened to me.

This is the London-based
[trio](https://open.spotify.com/artist/1N2FgBLehaq77UEdJhCt7f)'s debut album,
and it had solid critical reception, but I think that reception over here
across the pond was more rare.

**[Spring](https://open.spotify.com/track/6O9kI064xkcbQyjrPfENze)** is another dance forward track,
**[Stoned to Say the Least](https://open.spotify.com/track/5nXdXjGqbvOsb3YLdvJrYY)** has a strong, dark, groove, and
**[London Belongs to Me](https://open.spotify.com/track/4gPQcBwKmBygkO46msJ4Xn)** is the most 60's girl pop sounding song.

It's a really fun one to listen to front to back.
</Album>
